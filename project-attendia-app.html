Attendance Tracking Mobile Client App ("Attendia")

View Full Project

Attendia Mobile App is a modern mobile solution designed to digitize and simplify human resources operations. It enables HR teams and employees to manage attendance, leave requests, employee records, and communication through an intuitive, mobile-first interface that improves efficiency and enhances the overall workplace experience.

Many organizations rely on manual processes or outdated systems for HR tasks, leading to time-consuming administration, tracking errors, poor communication, and limited access to HR services outside the office. These fragmented tools make it difficult to keep employee data organized and HR operations running smoothly.

The app provides a centralized, user-friendly platform where employees can access HR services and HR teams can monitor workforce data in real time. With features like attendance tracking, leave management, digital employee records, and a clear HR dashboard, the solution streamlines processes, improves transparency, and supports a more connected and efficient workplace.

Key Technical Features

  • Intuitive dashboard displaying employee stats and alerts
  • Clean team and role views for easy navigation
  • Task and notice panels for streamlined HR operations
  • User profile and inbox modules for engagement and communication
